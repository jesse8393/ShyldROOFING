Premium Materials

Your siding is one of the first things people notice about your home. It also plays a major role in protecting your walls from moisture, wind, and temperature swings. When siding starts to warp, crack, or fade, it's more than a cosmetic issue.

At Shyld Roofing, we handle siding installation with the same care we bring to our roofing work. We take the time to do it right so your home looks great and stays protected for years to come.

When Does Your Siding Need Replacing?

Siding doesn't last forever. Here are some signs it might be time for new siding.

  • Warping, buckling, or panels pulling away from the wall
  • Cracks, holes, or visible damage from storms or impact
  • Fading, discoloration, or paint that won't hold
  • Moisture or mold behind the panels
  • High energy bills from poor insulation performance
  • Outdated look that no longer matches your home's style

Our Siding Installation Process

Free Inspection: We look at your existing siding, check for moisture damage behind the panels, and talk through your options. No charge, no obligation.

Written Proposal: You get a clear estimate with materials, labor, timeline, and total cost. No surprises.

Material Selection: Choose from vinyl siding, James Hardie fiber cement, or board and batten. We'll help you pick the right fit for your home's style and your budget.

Professional Install: Our crew removes the old siding, inspects and repairs the sheathing if needed, installs moisture barrier, and fits your new siding with precision.

Cleanup and Walkthrough: We clean up the job site and walk you through the finished work to make sure you're happy with everything.

Materials We Install

We work with trusted siding brands including James Hardie fiber cement, premium vinyl systems, and board and batten options. Each material has its own strengths and we're happy to walk you through the differences so you can make the best choice for your home.

What It Costs

Siding costs depend on the size of your home, the material you choose, and the condition of the existing walls. Vinyl is the most affordable option, James Hardie fiber cement is mid-range with excellent durability, and board and batten is a premium choice. We provide exact pricing in your written proposal so you know exactly what to expect.
