Premium Materials

Your roof is the single most important structural element protecting your home. When it's time for a full replacement, choosing the right contractor and materials makes the difference between a roof that barely gets by and one that stands strong for 30+ years through Middle Tennessee's storms, heat, and humidity.

At Shyld Roofing, we approach every roof replacement the same way — thorough inspection, honest assessment, premium materials, and installation practices that exceed manufacturer specifications. We don't cut corners because we live here too, and our reputation depends on every single roof we install.

When Does Your Roof Need Replacing?

Not every roofing issue requires a full replacement. But when multiple warning signs stack up, replacement becomes the smarter investment over repeated repairs.

  • Shingles that are curling, cracking, buckling, or missing granules
  • Visible daylight coming through the roof deck from inside the attic
  • Multiple active leaks or water stains on interior ceilings
  • Roof age exceeding 20 years for standard architectural shingles
  • Sagging or uneven rooflines indicating structural deck damage
  • Rising energy bills caused by poor attic ventilation and insulation breakdown

Our Roof Replacement Process

Free Inspection: We climb the roof, inspect the deck, check attic ventilation, photograph everything, and walk you through our findings. No charge, no obligation.

Written Proposal: You receive a detailed estimate covering materials, labor, timeline, warranty information, and total cost. No hidden fees.

Material Selection: Choose from premium architectural shingles (GAF Timberline, Owens Corning Duration, CertainTeed Landmark), standing seam metal, or exposed fastener metal systems.

Full Tear-Off & Install: We remove every layer down to the deck, inspect and repair damaged decking, then install ice and water shield, synthetic underlayment, starter strips, new shingles or metal panels, ridge vent, and new flashing at all penetrations.

Cleanup & Final Walk: We use tarps and magnets to protect your landscaping and catch every nail. Your property will be cleaner than when we arrived.

Materials We Install

We only install products from manufacturers with proven track records in the Southeast climate — GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed for shingles, and premium metal panel systems for standing seam and exposed fastener installations. Every system includes proper underlayment, ice and water shield, ridge ventilation, and flashing.

What It Costs

Roof replacement costs across Middle Tennessee typically range from \$8,000 to \$20,000+ for residential homes, depending on roof size, pitch, material choice, and deck condition. Metal systems run higher upfront but offer significantly longer lifespans. We provide exact pricing in your written proposal — never a ballpark that changes later.

FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a roof replacement take?+
Most residential roofs are completed in 1-2 days. Larger or more complex roofs may take 2-3 days. We give you an exact timeline before we start.
Do I need to be home during the replacement?+
No. Many of our customers go about their day while we work. We communicate via text throughout and do a walk-through when you are available.
Will you remove the old roof or go over it?+
We always recommend a full tear-off down to the deck. This lets us inspect for hidden damage and ensures the longest-lasting result.
What warranty do I get?+
You receive our written workmanship warranty plus the manufacturer material warranty (25-50 years depending on product). Both documented before we start.
Can you help with my insurance claim?+
Yes. We provide thorough documentation with photos and can work alongside your adjuster to ensure all legitimate damage is accounted for.
